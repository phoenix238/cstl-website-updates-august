Who It's For

Access to this work shouldn't depend on what you earn.

The people who most need to feel supported and at ease in their bodies are often the ones least able to pay for it. The community clinic exists to change that, offering full-length, full-quality low cost craniosacral therapy sessions on a sliding scale, with no proof required and no questions asked.

The clinic is especially for:

  • People who already know the value of this work but can't always afford it
  • Artists, dancers, musicians, and other creative workers
  • People on low or variable incomes
  • Young adults and teens finding their way
  • Anyone curious about CST who's been put off by full pricing

It's the same work I offer at full price. Same 60 minutes. Same presence. Same care. The only thing that changes is what you pay.

Why This Exists

I believe all forms of therapeutic work can be a powerful way to explore, deepen, and shift your relationship to life. But too often, it comes with a price that makes it inaccessible. The people who can't afford it are often the ones who would benefit most, not because they are lacking, but because access itself is part of the problem.

This clinic is especially for young adults and teens who are finding their way, who deserve support without having to sacrifice their financial stability to receive it. If more people had access to this kind of space, more people would know what it feels like to be genuinely regulated, supported, and at ease in themselves.

How It Works

To book, send an email or WhatsApp message asking about current availability. Sessions run weekly for most of the year, with some natural loosening of the schedule in summer.

Fees run from £30 to £60 across four tiers, based on your financial circumstances. No proof is needed. No questions are asked beyond the one I'm asking you to ask yourself: which tier honestly fits where I am right now?

The Space

The clinic runs from a dance studio in an artists' building on Cambridge Heath Road. It's a warm, creative, lived-in space rather than a clinical one, which I love. It also sits on a main road, so you'll sometimes hear traffic or the hum of the city outside, particularly around rush hour.

For most people that softens into the background once we begin. But if you're sensitive to sound, or would simply prefer more quiet, I keep earplugs on hand, just ask, and we'll make sure you're settled before we start.

The Fee Tiers
£30
For people not currently in paid work, or whose income is genuinely limited. This includes those on benefits, between jobs, or in significant financial hardship.
£40
For people working part time, freelancing with an inconsistent income, or managing on a modest wage. If money is tight but you're earning something, this tier is likely for you.
£50
For people in regular full-time employment with a stable income who still feel the financial pressure of living in London.
£60
For people in regular full-time employment with a stable income and no significant financial pressures.

If you're genuinely unsure which tier fits, feel free to get in touch and we can have a brief, honest conversation.
